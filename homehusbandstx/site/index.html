The specialty · Chemical-free

Critter proofing, done right: we seal — we don’t spray.

Rodents don’t need an open door — just a gap. A house mouse fits through a hole the size of a dime, and every Texas home has a dozen of them. We find each one and seal it shut with carpentry-grade materials, so critters can’t get back in — no poison, no chemicals, no monthly spray contract.

What we seal

  • Foundation gaps & weep holes

    Steel mesh and weep-hole covers close the dime-sized openings mice walk straight through.

  • Roof lines, soffits & eaves

    Where the roof meets the wall is a highway in. We flash and mesh those gaps shut.

  • Pipe & utility penetrations

    Every line into the house leaves a gap around it. We pack and seal them with steel wool and sealant.

  • Vents & attic screens

    Dryer, gable, and attic vents get galvanized hardware-cloth screens that breathe but block.

  • Garage door corners & gaps

    The number-one way in. Corner seals and gap kits close the openings at the slab and the sides.

  • Exterior door sweeps

    A worn sweep leaves a quarter-inch gap under the door. We fit new ones that seal to the threshold.

Straight about what we do — and what we don’t.

Our critter proofing is completely chemical-free — we remove the critters and seal the structure behind them so they can’t get back in, with no poison, no sprays, and nothing toxic left in your home or dying in your walls. It’s hands-on carpentry and exclusion, not chemical extermination — and Texas doesn’t require a structural pest control license for chemical-free work like this.

Texas doesn’t issue a general handyman license either, and we don’t perform work reserved for licensed plumbing, electrical, HVAC, or irrigation contractors. Detector work is scoped as replace and test, put in writing — never sold as an inspection. Clear scope is how you avoid the surprises that make home services frustrating in the first place.

Questions homeowners actually ask

Do you use poison or chemicals?

No — our critter proofing is completely chemical-free. We remove the critters and seal the gaps they use to get in, with carpentry-grade materials like steel mesh, hardware cloth, and door sweeps. Nothing toxic in your home, nothing dying in your walls, no monthly spray contract.

What if critters are already inside?

We remove them and seal the structure behind them — chemical-free — so nothing gets back in. That’s the permanent fix: get them out, then close every gap they used, for good.

How long does the seal hold?

Because it’s physical sealing and not a treatment that wears off, a proper exclusion lasts for years — and we back it with a seal guarantee. If something we sealed opens back up, we come fix it.

How soon can you come out?

Most one-off jobs are booked within two to three days. Plan visits run on a quarterly cycle — and we set the next date before we leave, so it never slips.
