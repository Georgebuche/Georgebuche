Questions we get asked

How much does bidet installation cost in Tomball, TX?

A flat $149, and that includes the bidet itself and all supplies. There is no separate parts charge and no trip fee.

Do I need a plumber to install a bidet?

For a standard bidet seat or attachment on an existing toilet, no. It connects to the toilet's existing water supply line with a T-valve — no new plumbing, no cutting into walls, no permit. A typical install takes about 45 minutes.

Does a bidet need an electrical outlet?

A non-electric bidet does not — it runs on your household water pressure alone. Heated-seat and warm-water models do need a grounded outlet within reach of the toilet. Tell us which type you want when you call and we will confirm what your bathroom needs before we arrive.

What is included in a quarterly home maintenance visit?

Five things, every visit, for $199: A/C and vent filters changed, smoke alarm batteries replaced and every alarm tested, the A/C condensate drain line flushed, the garbage disposal reset and checked, and an attic inspection covering insulation, moisture and signs of pests.

How often should I flush my A/C condensate drain line in Texas?

Every three months during cooling season. Gulf Coast humidity means the line runs wet most of the year and algae builds up fast. A clogged line backs water into the pan and then through the ceiling — which is exactly why it is on the quarterly list and not the annual one.
