## Reviews — the thing that actually decides it

A **4.3★ average across three platforms beats 5.0★ on one.** Spread them deliberately: send the first few customers to Google, the next to Yelp, the next to Nextdoor.

Text to send the same day the job is done, while they're still pleased:

> Thanks for having me out today. If the bidet's working the way you wanted, would you mind leaving a quick review? It's the main way people around Tomball find us. Takes about 30 seconds: [link]

Ask every single customer. Ask on the day. Name the platform and give the direct link — "leave us a review" with no link converts at close to nothing.

## Why this business is unusually well-suited to AI search

Most handymen say "quality work, free estimates" and are uncitable — there is no fact in that sentence for an assistant to repeat.

You have three things that are specific, priced and quotable:

- **"Bidet installation, $149, bidet and supplies included."** Low-competition query, high intent, and a complete answer. When someone asks an assistant "how much does bidet installation cost near Tomball", a page that states a flat number including the unit is exactly what gets pulled.
- **A five-item checklist at a fixed $199.** Enumerable, comparable, repeatable.
- **A service you explicitly decline to do** (trapping, bats). Stated limits read as expertise, and they are the kind of detail that gets a business quoted rather than summarized.

That's why the pages lead with the number and the answer before any selling. Keep that discipline in anything you add later.
